# The Dealey Plaza Files

A classroom investigation about the assassination of President John F. Kennedy, for middle school (1-2 class periods).

Students explore conspiracy theories first, as claims to examine. The fact-check stays locked until the teacher gives the class a passcode.

## How it works
1. **Start:** instructions. Students choose 2 routes, with bonus routes if they finish early.
2. **Case File:** what nearly everyone agrees on, a simplified map, a timeline, words to know.
3. **Investigate:** 8 routes, each with claim cards, evidence ratings, source-check questions, and videos.
4. **Reflect:** what made the theories feel convincing.
5. **Fact-Check (locked):** what the evidence shows, open questions, and a thinking toolkit.
6. **My Answers:** answers compiled for students to copy.

## Routes
The Magic Bullet, The Grassy Knoll, The Umbrella Man, Oswald the Patsy, Who Had a Motive?, The Cover-Up, The Doctors vs. the Autopsy, Jack Ruby and the Mystery Men.

## Running it
It is a single self-contained file. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages.

- YouTube videos are embedded, and each one has a fallback "open on YouTube" link in case the embed is blocked.
- Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Students copy their answers from the My Answers tab.
- Some videos and the Doctors route discuss the shooting and injuries. Every video has a content notice and a "Read instead" summary.
